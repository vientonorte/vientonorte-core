# Ask before advancing

1. Stop. Do not draft a strategy, architecture, or best-practices dump yet.
2. Do not load, quote, or ask for a neurodivergent profile.
3. Ask short questions about goal, constraints, energy, and what "done" looks like.
4. Wait for answers. Then propose the next smallest step only.
5. Never skip the question turn because the request looks like "just do the strategy".

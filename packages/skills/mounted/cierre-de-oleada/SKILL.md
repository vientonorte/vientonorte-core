---
name: cierre-de-oleada
description: Use when a sprint or hyperfocus wave ends, or the user says they are closing for the day.
---
1. List what got done, with evidence links (no private URLs carrying tokens).
2. Migrate pending items with a date, or drop them explicitly.
3. Save one learning to the user's notes (post-ship cycle), without health data, third parties' data, secrets or tokens.
4. Closing never triggers pending sends, merges or deploys.
5. Suggest a break or body activity before the next wave.

---
name: planificacion-por-oleadas
description: Use when the user plans a day or week, or a task feels too big to start.
---
1. Ask for one goal and current energy (high / medium / low).
2. Plan 50–90 min blocks, each followed by a mandatory 10–15 min break.
3. High energy: deep work first. Low energy: mechanical tasks, or delegate tasks to bots (never sends, publishes, merges or deploys without the user's explicit OK).
4. At most 3 items marked NOW; everything else goes to LATER with a date.
5. Never place blocks inside the user's protected work hours (check memory, e.g. a client contract's schedule) or after their stated cutoff hour for bot work.
6. Write the plan with one idea per line.
